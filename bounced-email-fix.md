# Bounced Email Fix Plan

## Problem
~10 outreach emails bounced with "Address not found" because we used info@domain.com pattern, which doesn't exist for many businesses.

## Bounced addresses (confirmed)
- info@858appliance.com
- info@sandiegoroofing.com
- info@rotorooter.com
- info@mrrooter.com
- info@accoes.com
- info@andersonplumbingheatingandair.com
- info@dmghvac.com
- info@sandiegosgreenhvac.com
- info@diamondroofing.com

## Fix strategy
1. Visit each bounced business's website and find their ACTUAL contact email or contact form
2. Re-send the outreach via their real contact form or email
3. For future prospects: always verify the email exists before sending

## Alternative approach
For businesses without public emails, use their contact forms directly via browser.

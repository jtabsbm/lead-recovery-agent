# Secondary Zero-Cash Opportunity Lanes

These lanes can create cash, proof, or introductions, but they are not the core path to a stable $1,000/day profit. Availability and eligibility change, so verify each official rule page before spending time.

## 1. Paid hackathons and competitions

Use only events with:

- an official organizer and published rules;
- a cash or contract prize with clear payout terms;
- no required entry fee or paid subscription;
- a judging rubric you can satisfy in a weekend;
- permission to reuse the demo as a portfolio artifact.

Avoid events that require paid credits, speculative token purchases, or handing over broad rights without reading the terms. Treat prize money as uncertain and potentially taxable.

## 2. Public bug bounties

Only test assets that are explicitly in scope and follow the program's rules. Never scan random sites or attempt unauthorized access. Start with documentation/triage issues if you have the skills; do not claim expertise you do not have.

## 3. Paid research, evaluation, and task platforms

Check:

- country eligibility;
- payout method and minimum withdrawal;
- task availability, screening, and quality rules;
- whether work product or recordings contain confidential data;
- whether the effective hourly rate is worth the time.

Never create duplicate accounts or automate activity against platform rules.

## 4. Agent/task packages

Sell an outcome to a business rather than a generic “AI agent.” Examples:

- intake-to-spreadsheet cleanup for a property manager;
- weekly competitor/lead monitoring report for a local service firm;
- quote-request triage with human approval;
- FAQ and internal knowledge-base cleanup;
- review-response draft queue with owner approval.

Use free/open-source tools for a demo, then move production work into the client's accounts. Charge for setup and an outcome-based monthly service.

## Screening score

Score each opportunity from 0–2 on:

- clear official rules
- zero cash required
- legal/authorized access
- payout certainty
- time-to-cash
- reusable portfolio value

Do not pursue anything scoring under 8/12 unless the learning value is unusually high.

## Allocation rule

Until recurring client revenue exists:

- 70% of work time: direct sales and delivery for CallbackOps.
- 20%: building proof, referrals, and agent/task packages.
- 10%: verified competitions, bounties, and paid task opportunities.

The target is reached by collected, repeatable revenue—not by counting a possible prize as income.

# CallbackOps — Offer and Sales Math

## One-sentence offer

> We help home-service companies recover missed calls and web leads by responding quickly, qualifying the request, and booking or routing the next step—using the tools they already own.

## Best starting niche

Start with **HVAC, plumbing, or electrical contractors** in one metro. Choose a niche where:

- one booked job is worth enough that a monthly service fee is easy to compare;
- the owner can approve a pilot without a large procurement process;
- missed calls and after-hours inquiries are common;
- emergency or safety issues can be handed to a human immediately.

Do not start with medical, legal, financial, or other regulated workflows until you understand the applicable rules and have appropriate client controls.

## What the client receives

1. A simple lead-status board (client-owned spreadsheet or CRM view).
2. A response script for missed calls and web leads, with approved business hours and service area.
3. A qualification checklist: job type, location, urgency, preferred time, contact details, and exclusions.
4. A booking or callback workflow using the client's calendar, phone, inbox, or CRM.
5. Daily exception reporting and a weekly conversion summary.
6. A 14- or 30-day baseline report: lead count, response time, qualified conversations, booked appointments, and unresolved leads.

The product is a **reliable operating process**, not an unbounded promise of revenue.

## Pricing ladder

### Founding pilot — first 3 clients

- $1,500 setup, due after the first agreed verification event, plus $1,500/month.
- One service area, one intake path, one booking workflow, one weekly review.
- Client pays any paid phone/SMS/CRM usage directly.

### Standard

- $2,500/month plus any client-owned tool fees.
- Setup is quoted separately after the workflow is understood.
- 30-day initial term, then month to month.

### Premium

- $4,000/month for multiple locations, multiple intake channels, and faster reporting/response coverage.
- Only sell this after the workflow is repeatable and the client has a clear ROI measurement method.

Pricing is a proposal, not a guarantee of acceptance. Never represent a pilot as risk-free or claim that every recovered lead becomes a paying job.

## $1,000/day target

- $30,000/month / $2,500 per client = **12 standard clients**.
- The $1,000/day figure is a 30-day average, before taxes and the operator's labor.
- Until retention is proven, count only collected recurring revenue, not signed proposals.
- If a client requires paid software, deduct it from the contribution margin or have the client pay the vendor directly.

## Proof without fake testimonials

Before the first client:

- use a labeled sample audit based on public information;
- show the exact log fields and weekly report the client will receive;
- run a live role-play with the owner;
- offer a narrowly scoped pilot with a defined baseline.

After a client pays:

- get written permission before using their name, logo, screenshots, or numbers;
- record baseline and post-pilot metrics;
- ask for a short testimonial only if the client is satisfied.

## 10-minute discovery call

1. How do calls and web inquiries arrive today?
2. What happens when a call is missed or arrives after hours?
3. Who responds, and how quickly?
4. What does a qualified appointment look like?
5. What areas, job types, or emergencies should be excluded?
6. Where should booked appointments land?
7. What number would make a 14-day pilot obviously worth continuing?
8. Who approves scripts and customer-facing messages?

## Objections

**“We already have a receptionist.”**

> Great—this is a recovery and overflow layer, not a replacement. We can route anything uncertain to your existing team and show where handoffs happen.

**“I do not want an AI talking to customers.”**

> The pilot can be human-reviewed and manual-first. Automation is optional and only used for approved reminders and drafts.

**“Can you guarantee more jobs?”**

> No. I can guarantee the logging, response workflow, and reporting we agree to. We measure qualified conversations and booked appointments so you can decide from your own data.

**“We cannot pay for another tool.”**

> We can begin with the tools you already own and a shared tracking sheet. Any paid add-on is optional and stays in your account.

## Minimum viable contract terms

Use a plain-language order form that states:

- scope and hours;
- client approvals and response expectations;
- client data ownership;
- who pays for third-party tools;
- no guarantee of revenue or appointment volume;
- cancellation and payment terms;
- permission and security boundaries;
- handling of emergencies and sensitive requests.

Have a qualified professional review the contract for the relevant jurisdiction if the work becomes material.

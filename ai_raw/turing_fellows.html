Ideal candidate

Built for domain experts interested in AI

We select Frontier Fellows based on ambition and craft.

  • Interested in learning how AI is built and applying AI in their work
  • Works as a senior operator, leader, or researcher across software engineering, medicine, law, finance, STEM, or other high-impact fields
  • Has 5+ years honing their craft and wants to up-level with AI
  • Leads and collaborates across multiple cross-functional teams
  • Has entrepreneurial drive and a genuine passion for frontier technology
  • Can dedicate ~1-2 hours max a week to virtual fireside chats and workshops

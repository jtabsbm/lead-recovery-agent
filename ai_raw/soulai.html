Got a Quick Question?

Haven’t received an invite yet?

You can request an invite anytime. We’ll evaluate your profile, and if you meet our criteria, you’ll receive an official invitation. Even if not selected immediately, your request keeps you in consideration for future opportunities.

Is this network only for AI professionals?

No, we welcome experts from diverse fields and with a wide range of skills who can contribute to training and evaluating AI models.

What skills are most valued for this network?

Skills such as analytical thinking, attention to detail, strong reasoning, writing ability, creativity, and critical thinking are highly valued. Specific requirements vary depending on the project type.

How long will it take for me to receive my invite?

It depends on your profile and the supporting information you provide. All applications remain on our waitlist until they are shortlisted. If you are shortlisted, we will contact you for training and assessment before placing you on paid projects.

What makes this network different?

Exclusivity, quality, and impact. You’ll work alongside the top 1% of talent, on high-stakes projects shaping AI worldwide, with continuous learning opportunities and reliable payouts.

How will I be paid, and how often?

Payments vary by project: some are task-based, others hourly, and a few use a combined model. All payment details, rates, and cycles are shared upfront. Payouts are processed biweekly.

How soon will I get paid after completing work?

We have two payment cycles each month:
Cycle 1: Processed during the first week (on or after the 2nd).
Cycle 2: Processed during the third week (on or after the 17th).

# Outreach and Lead-List SOP

## Zero-cost prospecting rules

- Use public business information only.
- Personalize every message with one observable detail from the business's own website or public listing.
- Do not pretend to be a customer, employee, or vendor.
- Do not send bulk messages or scrape private data.
- Respect opt-outs and local email/telemarketing rules.
- Keep a suppression list and do not re-contact an opted-out business.

## Prospect record fields

- business name
- metro
- niche
- public website
- public phone
- public email/contact form URL
- owner/manager name if publicly listed
- observation (specific and factual)
- likely lead-recovery gap
- first contact date
- channel
- response
- next step
- opt-out flag

## Personalized email / contact-form message

Subject: quick missed-call idea for {{BUSINESS}}

Hi {{NAME}},

I was looking at {{BUSINESS}} while researching {{METRO}} {{NICHE}} companies. I noticed {{SPECIFIC_PUBLIC_OBSERVATION}}.

I build a small missed-lead recovery workflow: when a call or web inquiry is missed, the lead is logged, the approved response is sent, and the next step is booked or routed to your team. It can start with your existing tools and human review—no ads and no software purchase required to test the process.

Would a 10-minute review of your current missed-call flow be useful? If it is not relevant, reply “no” and I will not follow up.

James Thompson
absbm14@gmail.com

## Short phone opener

“Hi, this is {{YOUR_NAME}}. I help local {{NICHE}} companies recover missed calls and web inquiries. I am not calling to sell ads. Could I ask who handles missed-call follow-up, and whether a short workflow audit would be useful?”

If they say the owner is unavailable:

“Thanks—what is the best public way to send a two-minute overview? I will keep it to one message.”

## Follow-up 1 — 2 business days later

Hi {{NAME}},

Following up once on the missed-call workflow idea. The only reason I reached out is {{SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION}}. I can send a one-page sample audit showing exactly what I would measure, with no access to your systems.

If this is not a priority, just say so and I will close the loop.

{{YOUR_NAME}}

## Follow-up 2 — final

Last note from me. I am closing this out unless missed-call recovery is worth discussing. If you want the sample audit later, reply “audit.” Otherwise, no response needed.

{{YOUR_NAME}}

## Audit template

**Business:** {{BUSINESS}}

**Public observation:** {{FACTUAL_OBSERVATION}}

**Possible leak:** {{MISSED_CALL_OR_INTAKE_RISK}}

**Pilot measurement:**

- inbound calls/inquiries
- missed or unanswered items
- time to first response
- qualified conversations
- booked appointments
- unresolved items

**Proposed first change:** {{ONE_SMALL_WORKFLOW_CHANGE}}

**Boundary:** this is a sample based on public information; it is not a claim about your internal operations.

## Daily sales cadence

- 10 new personalized prospects
- 5 follow-ups
- 5 live-call attempts during appropriate business hours
- 1 short audit or role-play
- update the pipeline before stopping

If reply rate is low, improve the observation and niche fit before increasing volume. If replies are high but calls do not close, improve the offer and discovery call.

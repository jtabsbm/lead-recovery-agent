Hackathon Tracks

## Hackathon Tracks

Participants of the AI Infra Summit Hackathon choose a partner-led Challenge Track to build in. Onsite tracks require assignment ahead of time — online tracks are open build. This is your chance to build with real infrastructure and technology directly from the partner running the track. 🚀

Track Selection Process:

1

**Create Your Team.** Form your team as soon as possible. Onsite track assignment is only made for participants who are part of a team. Head over to the lablab.ai Discord server to connect with others — we'll also run Team Match-Making rounds ahead of the hackathon.

2

**Submit Your Team's Onsite Track Preference.** Only required for teams pursuing an **onsite** track. Team leaders submit the Track Selection Form on behalf of the team, listing all members and their preferred onsite track. Each team will receive confirmation of their assigned track.

3

**One Onsite Track Per Team.** Teams building an onsite challenge may only be assigned to **one** onsite track. Online tracks don't require assignment — build with any online track's technology directly.

4

**Onsite Eligibility.** Only teams working within their assigned onsite track are eligible for onsite participation at the AI Infra Summit.

⚠️ TBD — exact onsite capacity, the Track Selection Form link, and the submission deadline are still being finalized and will be communicated before the hackathon.

### Onsite Tracks

Requires Assignment

![Image 6: Intel](https://cdn.builder.io/api/v1/image/assets%2F972274c44a2d4c658b0fa440848d24a1%2F92def5e37d0a471ea85be5025c13dc2b)

Autonomous Sorting with Voice Commands

Train a dual SO-101 robot arm (leader/follower teleoperation) to sort objects into bins on spoken commands — e.g. "put all red items into bin A" — and add a defect-detection module to flag and discard visually damaged objects using camera input. Trained models deploy to Intel Panther Lake for a live demonstration.

Duration

2 days (~8h/day)

Difficulty

Intermediate–Advanced

Team Size

Flexible, ~10 teams

Hardware Provided

Intel NUC, SO-101 arms, RealSense 415 cameras

**Recommended toolkits:** Intel OpenVINO, Open Edge Platform, and Intel's physical AI / deep-learning libraries.

Dual-Arm Dinner Table Setup

Simulate two SO-101 arms in MuJoCo performing bimanual manipulation — handing off plates, opening drawers, pouring between arms. You're free to define your own objects, environments, and lighting to demonstrate simulation depth.

Judging Criteria

## How Projects Will Be Evaluated

Submissions will be scored across four dimensions by our panel of judges from across the AI infrastructure ecosystem.

Application of Technology

How effectively the chosen model(s) are integrated into the solution.

Presentation

The clarity and effectiveness of the project presentation.

Business Value

The impact and practical value, considering how well it fits into business areas.

Originality

The uniqueness & creativity of the solution, highlighting approaches and ability to demonstrate behaviors.

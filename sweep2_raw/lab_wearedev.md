## WeAreDevelopers Hackathon

A hackathon alongside WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America

The on-site days run alongside **WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America**, which gathers over 10,000 builders, more than 500 speakers and over 20 stages, with a program built around how teams design, ship, run, secure and test software in the AI era.

You can take part **entirely online**. Taking part on site in San José requires approval, and comes with additional prizes. Hackathon participants receive a ticket to WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America.

### WeAreDevelopers

WeAreDevelopers runs developer events and community programs worldwide. Its World Congress North America takes place September 23–25, 2026 in San José, California, bringing together developers, engineering leaders and the teams running AI systems in production.

The program spans building with AI, agentic systems in production, modern languages and runtimes, platform engineering and developer experience, cloud and reliability, data pipelines, security, testing and engineering leadership — across more than 20 stages, alongside a tech expo and a Builders Zone for people who would rather be building than watching.

Guidelines
## Guidelines

How to participate

The hackathon runs in two phases: an online phase from September 18 to 24, 2026, and on-site days at WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America in San José, California, on September 23–25, 2026. The online phase takes place on the lablab.ai platform and the [lablab.ai Discord server](https://discord.gg/lablabai). Please register for both in order to participate. To join, click the Enroll button at the bottom of the page and read the [Hackathon Guidelines](https://lablab.ai/ai-articles/hackathon-guidelines), the [Getting Started Guide](https://lablab.ai/getting-started-guide) and the [lablab.ai Hackathon Rule Book](https://lablab.ai/hackathon-rules).

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of previous AI or coding experience.

You can take part entirely online. Participants receive a ticket to WeAreDevelopers World Congress North America.

**Taking part on site in San José requires approval.** Participants approved for the on-site days are eligible for additional prizes. The approval process and what happens on site will be published on this page before the kickoff.

Submissions close on **September 24, 2026 at 5:00 PM PDT** — that is 2:00 AM CEST on September 25 for participants in Central Europe.

Before the kickoff, browse the [AI Tech](https://lablab.ai/tech) and [tutorials](https://lablab.ai/t) pages to read up on the available technologies and get a head start on your project.

Teams

Teams consist of 1-6 people. If you don't have a team, don't worry. You can connect with other participants from all over the world on the dashboard or on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/lablabai), where you can also find teammates and bounce around ideas.

Judging criteria

#### Application of Technology

How effectively the chosen model(s) are integrated into the solution.

#### Presentation

The clarity and effectiveness of the project presentation.

#### Business Value

The impact and practical value, considering how well it fits into business areas.

#### Originality

The uniqueness and creativity of the solution, highlighting approaches and ability to demonstrate behaviors.

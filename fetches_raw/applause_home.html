What is Applause, exactly?

Applause is a fully managed testing service — not a tool your team has to run. We created the crowdtesting category in 2007, bringing real-world human insight into the quality equation. Today, we combine our global testing community with enterprise-grade AI and automation to help organizations validate products in real-world conditions — faster, at scale, and with greater confidence.

When should a company use Applause?

Applause is most valuable when global teams are shipping fast but lack confidence in real-world quality — whether you're expanding into new markets, launching AI-driven features, struggling to reproduce issues, or finding that automation alone is missing critical bugs and user experience gaps.

How is Applause different from test automation and AI tools?

AI and automation are powerful — but knowing how and when to use them is what matters.

Applause brings the expertise to orchestrate AI, automation and human testing together — so you get the right coverage, in the right places, without managing the tools yourself. We integrate directly with your existing apps, SDLC and workflows to help you move quickly.

To clarify, does Applause use AI in its testing methods or test AI-infused applications?

Applause does both.

We evaluate AI-powered applications to validate outputs, identify issues like hallucinations or bias, and ensure real-world performance. At the same time, we use AI within our testing process to prioritize risk, expand coverage and accelerate execution.

This combination — AI, automation and real-world testing — helps deliver faster, more complete validation than any single approach alone.

What types of products can Applause test?

Applause tests web, mobile, AI-powered applications and complex digital experiences, including payments, accessibility, localization and end-to-end customer journeys. Whether you need the testing of regional ads on various set top devices during the Olympics, to determine how easily in-vehicle technology connects to users' phones, or need finance experts to evaluate a new billing AI feature to reduce hallucinations, we have you covered. The more complex, the more value we deliver.

What results does Applause deliver?

Applause delivers clear, prioritized insights — including reproducible bugs, UX issues and real-world feedback across devices, environments and markets — so your team can act quickly and release with confidence. Customers use Applause to accelerate release cycles, expand coverage and reduce customer-facing issues. An independent IDC study found organizations achieved up to 30% faster testing cycles and significant gains in efficiency and quality.

How quickly can we get started?

We can start very quickly — whether kicking off with a pilot or complex project. Applause works with you to align on goals, environments, devices, markets and service needs so testing can launch on the right timeline. And we are available to test 24/7/365 to help ensure you get results when you need them.

How much does Applause cost?

Contracts are tailored based on complexity, solution mix and the scale of your needs to ensure you get the most value out of your investment. Learn more on our pricing page.

Is Applause secure for enterprise use?

Yes. Applause is trusted by global enterprises to securely test applications, systems and devices across the SDLC. We operate with SOC 2–aligned controls, encrypted data handling and strict access governance. Security, privacy and compliance are built into how we deliver every engagement.

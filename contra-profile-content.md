## Service Listing

**Title:** Missed-Call & Web-Lead Recovery for Home-Service Businesses

**Description:**

Every missed call and unanswered web form is a job you already paid to attract — walking away. I build a simple lead-recovery workflow that captures, qualifies, and routes those inquiries so they turn into booked appointments instead of lost revenue.

**What you get:**
- Lead-status board (uses your existing tools — no new software)
- Approved response script for missed calls and web inquiries
- Qualification checklist (job type, location, urgency, preferred time)
- Booking or callback workflow using your calendar/phone/CRM
- Daily exception report + weekly conversion summary
- 14-day baseline report with clear metrics

**How it works:**
1. We review your current intake flow in a 10-minute call
2. I set up a tracker and approved script using your existing tools
3. Missed leads get logged, qualified, and routed — human-reviewed at every step
4. You get daily and weekly reports showing exactly what's being recovered

**Perfect for:** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, appliance repair companies with 3-20 technicians who know they're losing leads but don't have time to fix it.

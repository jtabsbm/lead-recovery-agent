Questions owners ask

Clear boundaries make the pilot easier to trust.

Do you replace our receptionist?

No. The first version is a recovery and overflow layer. Uncertain requests are routed to the existing team, and the client approves every customer-facing script.

Can you guarantee more revenue?

No. The service guarantees a defined process and reporting cadence, not a specific number of jobs. The pilot makes the baseline and outcome visible so the owner can decide.

Do we need another software subscription?

Not to test the workflow. Start with the tools the business already owns and a client-approved tracking sheet. Add paid tools only when the client chooses to fund them.

What happens with emergencies or sensitive requests?

They pause the workflow and go to the designated human escalation contact. The service does not diagnose, quote, or invent availability.

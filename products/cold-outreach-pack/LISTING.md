## FAQ

**Q: What am I selling with these templates — do they only work for lead recovery?**
A: The structure (observation + small offer + easy exit) works for any B2B service sold to contractors: marketing, bookkeeping, software, scheduling tools, consulting. The trade-specific initial emails are written for a lead-recovery offer; adapt one paragraph to your service and the rest — sequence, observations, objections, tracker — carries over as-is.

**Q: Is this cold email legal?**
A: Cold B2B email is legal in the US under CAN-SPAM if you follow its rules (no deception, identify yourself, honor opt-outs). Canada and the EU are much stricter. Section 10 gives the plain-language overview — read it before sending anything, and treat it as a starting point, not legal advice.

**Q: How many emails can I send with this?**
A: As many as you can personalize. The rule is one true observation per message — that caps a honest operator at roughly 10–20 quality sends a day, which is exactly the daily cadence in Section 8. This pack deliberately does not teach bulk; volume without personalization gets you ignored and reported.

**Q: Will this work outside the five trades?**
A: The universal sequence and about 80% of the pack (tracker, audit, cadence, diagnostics, compliance) are trade-agnostic. The observation prompts are the trade-specific part — the pattern for writing your own is demonstrated five times.

**Q: What makes this different from free cold-email templates online?**
A: Three things: (1) it's adapted from a live working pipeline, not theory; (2) every trade edition includes observation prompts — what to actually look for — because the observation does the work, not the template; (3) it includes the diagnostics table, so when replies are low you know what to fix instead of swapping templates blindly.

**Q: Do I need any tools?**
A: A spreadsheet and an email account. That's it. The pack is deliberately tool-agnostic.

**Q: Refunds?**
A: If the pack isn't useful, email within 14 days for a full refund.

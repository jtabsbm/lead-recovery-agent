## FAQ

**Q: What trades is this for?**
A: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing primarily — but any business where customers call, fill out a form, or text and expect a fast human response: garage doors, appliance repair, landscaping, pest control, lockouts. The failure modes and the SOPs are the same.

**Q: I'm a solo operator with no office staff. Will this work for me?**
A: It's written for exactly that situation. The system is designed to be run by one busy person: the tracker takes 20 seconds per lead, the missed-call SOP is a phone in your pocket, and the cadence is five scheduled touches you can do from the truck between jobs.

**Q: Do I need to buy software?**
A: No. The entire system runs on a spreadsheet and your phone. If you later want to automate parts, Chapter 14 explains what's worth automating — and our companion AI Lead Agent Starter Kit covers the code side if you want it.

**Q: How is this different from sales books?**
A: This isn't about persuading strangers — it's about not dropping the people who already contacted you. It's an operations manual: SOPs, checklists, scripts, and a measurement method, not motivation.

**Q: You really don't quote any conversion statistics?**
A: Really. Most of the "78% of customers buy from the first responder" claims floating around can't be traced to a verifiable primary source, so we won't repeat them. Chapter 2 replaces them with arithmetic you can run on your own numbers in ten minutes — and your own baseline beats anybody's benchmark.

**Q: What if it doesn't work for my business?**
A: Chapter 4's baseline will tell you within 14 days whether you have a leak worth fixing — before you invest real time. And if the playbook itself isn't useful, email within 14 days of purchase for a refund.

**Q: Can I use this to offer lead recovery as a service to other businesses?**
A: Yes — Appendix C is the client handoff checklist and Appendix D is the discovery-question list, both from real client-facing operating docs. (If that's your path, the Cold Outreach Pack in our catalog is the companion for getting clients.)

**Q: What format is the book?**
A: PDF-style long-form document (delivered as PDF), plus the appendices are copy-paste friendly. Print Appendix A and put it by the phone.

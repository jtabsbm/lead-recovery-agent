The bounce problem nobody measures

Nearly 1 in 6 businesses (16%) published contact emails that didn't work — info@ addresses pointing at mailboxes that no longer exist. Every lead-gen dollar those businesses spend leaks at the inbox, and their attribution dashboards never show it: the lead "was delivered."

What an hour of delay costs

An emergency HVAC call in summer is a $400–$900 job. The first shop to answer wins it — callers rarely leave a second voicemail. If you miss 8 after-hours calls a week and close at half your normal rate on the ones you return next day, that's a five-figure monthly leak in most markets.

Methodology

55 businesses sampled from public Google Business Profiles across San Diego County. Each received one personalized inquiry from a real local address with opt-out language. Response times measured from send to first human reply. Data anonymized at business level.
